Le Zheng is currently a Master's student at Sun Yat-sen University's [Rapid Lab](http://lab.sysu-robotics.com/lab/index), supervised by Prof. [Hui Cheng](https://cse.sysu.edu.cn/teacher/ChengHui).\
He is also gaining industry research experience as a Research Intern at the Embodied AI Research Center, [Agibot](https://www.agibot.com/), where he works on dexterous manipulation algorithms under the mentorship of [Tianhao Wu](https://tianhaowuhz.github.io/) and [Jianlan Luo](https://jianlanluo.github.io/).\
Previously, he completed his undergraduate studies at Northeastern University. During his undergraduate years, he competed in the DJI RoboMaster University Championship ([RMUC](https://www.robomaster.com/en-US/robo/overview?djifrom=nav)) in both the 2022 and 2023 seasons. In 2023, he served as the Algorithm Group Leader for the [T-DT Lab](https://www.neutdt.cn/) team advised by Professor [Zhiguo Lu](http://www.me.neu.edu.cn/2015/1105/c3343a55919/page.htm), [Chong Liu](http://faculty.neu.edu.cn/%7EVrYjUj/zh_CN/index.htm), winning the Global Runner-up.
